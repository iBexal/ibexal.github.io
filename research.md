# Research and Projects

<h2 id="cannon-project">Maximising the Scientific Returns of Spectroscopic Surveys Using Machine Learning</h2>

My current research investigates how machine learning can be used to extract
stellar labels from archival spectroscopic surveys.

### Goals

- Improve the precision of stellar parameters
- Measure chemical abundances
- Make better use of archival spectra
- Investigate the structure and formation history of the Milky Way

### Data

My work involves large spectroscopic surveys including:

- AAOmega
- GALAH
- APOGEE
- S5

### Methods

I use machine-learning techniques including **The Cannon** to transfer
information from well-characterised training samples to archival spectra.
