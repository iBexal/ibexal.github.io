## Surveys and Collaborations

I am a member of multiple Galactic spectroscopic surveys and collaborations:
 - <a href="https://www.4most.eu/cms/science/galconsurv/">4MOST S1 Milky Way Halo Low Resolution Survey</a>
 - <a href="https://s5collab.github.io">Southern Stellar Stream Spectroscopic Survey</a> (S5) collaboration
 - The <a href="https://www.galah-survey.org">GALAH and GALAH-2</a> surveys
